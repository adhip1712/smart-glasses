# Privacy Policy - Visionary Nexus YouTube Skip Helper

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Visionary Nexus - YouTube Skip Helper is an optional browser extension that detects YouTube's own visible, enabled Skip Ads control and clicks it when available.

## Information collected
The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not collect browsing history, search history, account information, location, or analytics data.

## Website access
The extension runs only on `youtube.com` and `www.youtube.com`. It reads the page elements needed to identify YouTube's native Skip Ads button. It does not read video contents, comments, messages, or account details.

## Third parties
The extension does not send information to Visionary Nexus servers or other third parties.

## Changes
This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any material change should be reflected in the Chrome Web Store listing.

## Contact
For privacy questions, use the contact method provided on the official Visionary Nexus website or Chrome Web Store listing.
